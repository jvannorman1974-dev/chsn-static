How it works

How selling to us works, start to finish

  1. Day 1 — You reach out. Fill out the form with as much as you know about the house — address, condition, when you need to move. You don’t have to know everything, and there’s no obligation just for asking.
  2. Day 1 to 2 — Jason reviews it and calls you. He looks at the house himself, talks through your situation, and answers your questions. If keeping the house is still possible, he’ll say so first.
  3. Within a few days — Walk-through, on your schedule. Jason comes to the house, at a time that works for you. No cleaning, no fixing, no staging. He sees it as-is.
  4. Then — A written cash offer, explained in plain words. No pressure to sign on the spot. You get time to think it over and talk to anyone you trust.
  5. If you accept — You sign a purchase agreement. If the contract will be assigned to a cash buyer, that’s disclosed in writing in the agreement, the way Wisconsin requires. Your price and terms don’t change because of that.
  6. Next — Title and escrow. We open escrow at the title company, and they run a title search to find the mortgage payoff, back taxes, liens, and anything else on the house, so there are no surprises at closing.
  7. Before closing — A cash buyer partner may do one quick walk-through of their own, if one is involved.
  8. Closing — Aimed at 2 to 3 weeks from agreement, at the title company. You sign, you get paid, and it’s done. No fees from you, no closing costs from you, no repairs, no showings to the public.
  9. After closing — You move on your timeline from there, with the house sold as-is behind you.

Why we buy

We’ve seen complicated houses and complicated timing before.

The situation can be messy. The conversation does not have to be.

Inherited house

You may not want another house, another set of bills, or months of cleanup. A cash sale can turn the property into a clear estate decision.

Divorce

When both sides need a clean property decision, a straightforward sale can create a closing date and proceeds to divide according to your agreement.

Tired landlord

Bad tenants, deferred maintenance, and nonstop calls can wear anyone down. We buy rentals in imperfect condition and work through the occupancy details.

Major repairs

Roof, foundation, water, mold, or a full interior project may be more than you want to take on. We can price the repairs into an as-is offer.

Job loss or relocation

When income changes or a move cannot wait, a cash sale can trade listing uncertainty for a defined closing plan.

Open or expired permits

Unfinished work and old permits can scare off retail buyers. We can evaluate the property as it is and account for the work that remains.

Any reason at all

You do not need a crisis to sell. If you want to sell, we will look at the facts and make a fair cash offer you are free to accept or decline.
