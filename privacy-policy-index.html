What we collect

  • Information you submit through our forms: your name, phone number, email address, property address, and anything you tell us about the property or your situation.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request, discuss your options, evaluate a property, and follow up where you asked us to.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only where needed to run the website and its forms (for example, the form service that delivers your submission to us by email), or where required by law. We do not share it for third-party marketing.

Text (SMS) alerts

Our SMS program sends account notifications to the business owner when a new homeowner request or cash-buyer signup comes in on this website. The owner opts in by entering his own mobile number in the website dashboard settings and turning on SMS notifications.

  • Message frequency varies with form activity — only when a new submission arrives.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out of SMS alerts at any time. Reply HELP for help.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We do not send marketing text messages to website visitors.
